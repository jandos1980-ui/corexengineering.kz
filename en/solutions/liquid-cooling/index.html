Liquid cooling for AI and HPC

GPU server density changes heat rejection requirements. We develop cold plate, CDU and immersion cooling configurations around the server platform and site resources so infrastructure does not constrain AI/HPC deployment.

Discuss AI/HPC cooling ↗

When this is needed

For AI clusters, GPU servers and racks with high heat loads. Scenarios of 50–150 kW per rack require power and cooling calculations, server platform checks and assessment of external heat rejection.

Solution scope

01

Cold plates

Cold plates transfer component heat to a liquid loop. Selection against server platform requirements supports concentrated heat loads.

02

CDUs and cooling loops

A CDU connects the server loop to the external cooling supply. Coordinating temperature, flow and heat exchange helps maintain IT equipment operating conditions.

03

Immersion tanks

Immersion cooling submerges compatible equipment in a dielectric fluid. We assess heat rejection and maintenance procedures for practical operation.

04

Monitoring and operation

Leak detection and loop monitoring integrated with DCIM help operations teams identify deviations and act before an incident escalates.

What the client receives

A cooling concept with power, coolant and connection requirements as a basis for site preparation and infrastructure cost calculations.

Inputs for selection

GPU server models, heat load per rack, coolant parameters, external cooling supply and manufacturer requirements.

PUE < 1.2 is an efficiency target. Feasibility is assessed against load, climate and all data centre systems; savings are calculated for the specific site.
