COREX / DATA CENTERS

Turnkey data centres

A data centre must support business operations and growing IT loads. We design power, cooling and monitoring as an integrated system, from technical concept and direct supply to installation, commissioning and maintenance.

Discuss your data centre configuration ↗

When this is needed

For launching a site, expanding server capacity or migrating critical services. A coordinated architecture helps estimate investment before procurement and plan for growth.

Solution scope

01

Concept and design

We define load, site resources and redundancy. Maintainability and resilience requirements guide architecture and budget decisions.

02

Engineering infrastructure

We coordinate power, cooling, rack placement, monitoring and security, checking system interfaces so one system's capacity does not constrain the others.

03

Delivery and commissioning

Direct supply, installation and functional testing against project documentation. The client receives installed systems and documented test results for acceptance.

04

Ongoing support

Maintenance procedures and expansion plans reflect data centre operations, helping plan service and capital investment throughout its life.

What the client receives

An agreed architecture, system specifications, delivery stages and acceptance criteria as a basis for managing the project and its cost.

Inputs for selection

Site, IT capacity, rack count and density, availability requirements, schedule and growth plan.

Target Tier III/Tier IV levels and applicable ANSI/TIA-942-C and PCI DSS requirements are recorded in the specification. Facility compliance and certification require separate verification; system scope is defined by the project.
