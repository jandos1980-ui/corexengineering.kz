Equipment and capabilities

COOLNET

PGM

Modular UPS

  • PGM is a modular series; the catalogue lists 200–600 kVA configurations with an output power factor of 1. We select capacity for the load and required reserve.
  • N+X architecture, hot swapping and additional power modules simplify maintenance and phased capacity growth within the selected configuration.
  • Front access simplifies servicing. Battery and charging-current settings align runtime with load and recharge requirements.
COOLNET

Power Distribution Cabinet

Power distribution cabinet

  • Hall-level power distribution with consumption metering helps monitor circuit loading and spare capacity.
  • Electrical measurements delivered to monitoring give the operations team data for detecting deviations and planning new connections.
  • Ratings, outgoing circuits and interfaces are selected against the power design so distribution matches load and redundancy requirements.

PGH is a high-frequency online UPS range; PGM is a modular UPS range. Model, capacity, batteries and redundancy are determined by calculation; runtime depends on load.

Source: Coolnet 2026. PDF pages: 20, 11.

What CoreX delivers

01

Load and runtime

We size UPS systems and batteries for consumption and required runtime, aligning energy reserves with the transfer time to backup power and business operating needs.

02

Sources and distribution

We coordinate generators, automatic transfer switches, PDUs and rack power, checking that individual connections preserve the agreed redundancy design.

03

System testing

We test the interaction of sources, protection and distribution against an agreed programme. Test records support acceptance and future maintenance.

What the client receives

A power diagram and UPS, battery and distribution specifications with agreed capacity, runtime and expansion options.

Inputs for calculation

Load capacity and characteristics, required runtime, incoming supplies, generators and rack expansion plans.
