COOLNET / METACUBE

Infrastructure in a single container

MetaCube combines racks and infrastructure in a factory-built container. Pre-assembly and testing reduce on-site installation; the site and external connections are prepared before deployment.

  • Control and monitoring
  • Fire suppression
  • Cabling system
  • Raised floor
  • Cooling
  • Server cabinets
  • Cooling condenser
  • Power distribution
  • UPS

MetaCube configurations

Where MetaCube is used
  • Enterprise — server infrastructure close to production and office workloads.
  • Public sector — hosting IT systems for smart and safe cities.
  • Education — computing infrastructure for universities and research organisations.
  • Energy — hosting IT systems at production and exploration sites.
  • Finance — a backup site for recovering IT services after an incident.
  • Transport — IT infrastructure for airports, railways and ports without a separate server building.
  • Telecommunications — edge and backup data centres close to the workloads they serve.

Outdoor configurations are selected for the climate and location. IT capacity, redundancy and connections are calculated for the chosen specification; deployment timing depends on site readiness.

What CoreX delivers

01

Configuration matched to load

We select racks, power and cooling for the IT load and required reserve. Expansion is considered when choosing the format to support phased investment.

02

Site preparation

We check the site, power and external cooling supply and document supply and installation boundaries so readiness can be tracked before module delivery.

03

Testing and commissioning

We check completeness, connections and system operation and configure monitoring. Commissioning results support operational handover.

What the client receives

A module configuration, site requirements and connection scope as a basis for cost estimation and deployment preparation.

Inputs for calculation

Site purpose, rack count and density, available power, external connections, climate and expansion stages.
