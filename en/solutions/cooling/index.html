COREX / PRECISION COOLING

Precision cooling

Overheating and uneven airflow limit rack utilisation. We select precision cooling based on heat output, layout and redundancy to keep servers within design conditions and match system capacity to the load.

Discuss cooling selection ↗

Equipment and capabilities

COOLNET

Cyber Master

Room-level cooling

  • A room cooling series offering DX models rated at 20–120 kW or chilled-water versions at 30–200 kW. The range supports selection for the server hall.
  • Modular construction and airflow options help align cooling with the layout and rack arrangement.
  • We select models by total and sensible cooling capacity at design conditions to remove heat from the specified IT load.
COOLNET

Cool Row

In-row cooling

  • In-row placement shortens the airflow path between servers and cooling units, helping reduce local hotspots.
  • Total cooling capacity is 13.2–63.9 kW for CRA and 30.6 or 61.1 kW for CRW. Selection based on a heat map helps distribute cooling along the row.
  • CRA inverter models modulate capacity from 30–100%; EC fans respond to load changes. RS485 and leak alarms support condition monitoring.

Capacity and energy consumption are calculated for the selected model and operating mode. Free cooling is considered where the cooling system and site climate allow it.

Source: Coolnet 2026. PDF pages: 4, 5.

What CoreX delivers

01

Calculation and selection

Cool Smart 6–20 kW for small server rooms, Cool Row for rows and Cool Rack for in-rack cooling. We match the cooling level to the load to avoid hotspots and excessive installed capacity.

02

Installation and commissioning

We coordinate connections and placement, install and commission equipment, and test cooling as part of the infrastructure before operational handover.

03

Coolnet service

Authorised preventive maintenance, diagnostics and servicing of Coolnet precision air conditioners help maintain design conditions and plan repairs.

What the client receives

A cooling layout and equipment selection with capacity and redundancy rationale, supporting configuration comparisons and cost planning.

Inputs for calculation

Hall layout, heat output and rack density, cooling supply parameters, redundancy and operating conditions.
