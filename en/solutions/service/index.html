COREX / SERVICE & SUPPORT

Service and upgrades

Scheduled service helps identify wear and deviations before failure. CoreX is a qualified Coolnet service company providing installation, commissioning, diagnostics and maintenance of precision air conditioners and modular UPS systems.

Discuss your maintenance plan ↗

When this is needed

For round-the-clock operation, recurring faults or growing IT loads. Condition assessments help choose between maintenance, repair and upgrades.

Solution scope

01

Installation and commissioning

Authorised Coolnet installation and functional testing to manufacturer documentation. Checks before commissioning help identify connection and configuration errors.

02

Preventive maintenance

Maintenance aligned with prescribed procedures and site conditions supports design operating modes. Scheduled work allows service to be included in operational planning.

03

Diagnostics and repair

We identify fault causes and agree remedial work. Warranty and post-warranty support follows manufacturer requirements.

04

Upgrades

We compare system condition with future loads. Recommendations help focus investment on components that limit capacity or increase operational risk.

What the client receives

A maintenance plan, diagnostic scope and upgrade recommendations to support service and operating-cost planning.

Inputs for assessing the work

Models and serial numbers, maintenance history, fault symptoms, operating conditions and equipment access restrictions.

For 24/7 operation, support hours, response times and SLAs are agreed in the contract. Work scope depends on equipment condition and site requirements.
