A unified view of your infrastructure

What CoreX delivers

01

Monitoring points

We define measured parameters and critical nodes, focusing power, temperature and leak monitoring on risks to service operation.

02

Integration

We check protocols and connect compatible devices. A single interface reduces manual reconciliation of data from separate systems.

03

Events and alerts

We configure thresholds, logs and alerts for responsible staff. Agreed workflows help the operations team turn system signals into action.

What the client receives

A monitoring map, integration list and alert workflows as a basis for observing systems and planning available capacity.

Inputs for calculation

Equipment and protocol lists, existing monitoring, critical parameters and the operations team's response procedures.
